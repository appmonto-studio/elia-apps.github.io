Privacy

Elia Moments · Last updated 19 November 2026

You can use the app offline without an Elia Account. In local use, entries, history and settings are saved on your device.

Sharing and cloud storage

In a local archive, notes and selected photos stay on your device. In versions that support connecting to a family archive, connecting enables synchronization. Shared entries and supported photos are stored in the cloud and can be accessed by family members according to their roles. Online place suggestions also require a connection.

Backups and deletion

You choose where to save a manual backup or export. Clearing the app’s storage removes the local copy. Files you exported, summaries you sent and copies already stored in a connected account or family archive are separate. Contact us below for help with account data.

Permissions and tracking

Local recording works without an internet connection. The apps are designed for adults and caregivers. Elia does not sell your entries. The apps contain no ads or third-party tracking SDKs. Cloud features require a connection.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to moments@getelia.app.
